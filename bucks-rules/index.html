Raider Bucks rules

Last updated November 12, 2026

Raider Bucks are club points you earn by playing golf and showing up. Trade them for club gear. They're points, not money.

How you earn them

What you doBucks
Welcome bonus, once250
Post a round, up to 2 a day25
Play a club event50
Fantasy league weekly win20
Fantasy league weekly high score50

Fantasy Bucks only come from the club's free league. The club doesn't run paid fantasy leagues.

No cash value

  • Raider Bucks can't be bought. You can only earn them.
  • They can't be sold, traded for cash or gift cards, or cashed out.
  • They can't be moved to another member.
  • They're tied to your member account and have no value outside the club.

Side games in Bucks

  • You can play your round's side games for Bucks: skins, a Nassau or a custom game among the players in your round.
  • Golf games only. Never bets on NFL games or any other sport.
  • Up to 200 Bucks a round, and up to 300 Bucks in winnings a day.
  • A wallet can't go below zero.
  • The club never takes a cut. Winnings and losses post when the round is saved.

Trading for gear

Trade Bucks for club items on the Club store tab of your wallet. A club leader confirms the request and ships it. Items depend on stock. If an item runs out, your Bucks go back to your wallet.

Fair play

Bucks from fake rounds, scores you didn't play or a glitch can be removed. Club leaders can also correct a wallet after a mistake. Abuse can mean losing your Bucks or your account.

Changes to the program

The club can change how many Bucks things earn, what gear costs, or the rules, and can end the program. We'll give members at least 30 days' notice in the app before ending it, so you can trade what you have. Bucks left after the program ends have no value.

Other rules

Raider Bucks are part of the club's terms of use.
