Store policy

Last updated November 12, 2026

The club store sells memberships, event entries and club gear. Prices are in US dollars and come from the club, not the page.

Paying

Cards are processed on our payment company's secure page. We never see or store your full card number. Your order is confirmed once the payment company tells us it went through, and you get an order number on screen.

Memberships

A membership runs one year from the day you pay. Your chapter president will reach out with next steps. Memberships can be refunded within 14 days if you haven't used any member benefits yet, like an event spot or your bag tag.

Event entries

  • Your spot is held once your payment goes through.
  • Cancel 14 days or more before the event for a full refund.
  • Inside 14 days, entries can't be refunded because the club has already paid the course. You can hand your spot to another member with your chapter president's OK.
  • If the club cancels or moves an event, you get a full refund or a spot at the new date, your pick.

Shipping gear

Gear ships to the address you give at checkout. We ship within 30 days of your order. If we can't, we'll email you a new date and you can cancel for a full refund. Shipping is included while we set rates.

Wrong, damaged or missing

Email us within 30 days of delivery with your order number and a photo. We'll send the right item or refund you, including shipping.

Sizes and exchanges

Unworn gear with tags can be exchanged for another size within 30 days of delivery, while that size is in stock. You pay to ship it back; we pay to ship the new one. Items printed or made to order can't be returned unless they arrive wrong or damaged.

Refunds

Refunds go back to the card you paid with. Your bank can take 5 to 10 business days to show it.

Raider Bucks trades

Gear you get with Raider Bucks isn't part of a cash order. It follows the Raider Bucks rules.

Other policies

Store orders also follow our terms of use and privacy policy.
