Terms of use

Last updated November 12, 2026

By using the club app or website, you agree to these terms. The short version: be a good club member, play fair, and remember the club doesn't handle money between members.

Who we are

Raider Nation Golf Club ("the club," "we") is a fan club for Raiders fans who golf. We're an independent fan club, not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by the Las Vegas Raiders or the NFL. Team names belong to their owners.

Your account

  • You must be 18 or older.
  • Use your real name and keep your contact info current.
  • One account per person. Don't share your sign-in.
  • Tell us right away if you think someone else is using your account.

Membership

Memberships run one year from when you join. What each membership includes is listed in the club store. Payments, refunds and shipping follow our store policy.

Play fair

  • Post real scores for rounds you actually played.
  • No harassment, hate, threats or spam in posts, messages or listings.
  • Don't post anyone's private information.
  • Don't try to break into the app, other accounts or our servers.

Club leaders can fix bad scores, remove posts, and suspend or close accounts that break these rules.

Side games and money between members

The scorecard can keep a tally for skins, a Nassau or other side games. That's all it does: it keeps score. The club never collects, holds, pays out or takes a cut of side money, in the app or at events. Players settle among themselves and are responsible for following the laws where they play. Side games in the app are golf games among the players in your round, never bets on NFL games or any other sport.

Raider Bucks

Raider Bucks are club points with no cash value. They follow the Raider Bucks rules.

Ticket exchange

Members can list Raiders and club event tickets for each other. The club isn't part of any ticket deal. We don't hold tickets or money and can't guarantee a ticket or a payment. Transfer in the official ticket app and pay after the ticket lands. Sell at or below face value where your state requires it.

Events

Golf has real risks: flying balls, carts, heat and uneven ground. You take part in club events and rounds at your own risk and follow each course's rules. Event entries follow the store policy.

What you post

You keep ownership of the photos and posts you share. You give the club permission to show them in the app and on club pages and social media. Only post what you have the right to share.

The app as is

We work hard to keep scores, yardages and handicaps right, but the app is provided as is. GPS yardages are estimates. The official handicap is the one in GHIN. We may change, pause or stop features at any time.

Limits on our liability

To the extent the law allows, the club and its volunteers aren't liable for indirect or lost-profit damages from using the app, and our total liability to you is limited to what you paid the club in the 12 months before the claim.

California law

These terms are governed by California law. If there's a dispute, let's talk first: email us and we'll try to sort it out within 30 days.

Other club policies

Our privacy policy explains what we collect and who sees it.

Changes

We may update these terms. We'll change the date at the top, and tell members about big changes in the app or by email. Keeping using the app after a change means you accept it.
