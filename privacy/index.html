Privacy policy

Last updated November 12, 2026

We collect what it takes to run the club: who you are, how you play and what you buy. We don't sell it, we don't run ads, and we don't use tracking or analytics tools.

Who we are

Raider Nation Golf Club ("the club," "we") runs this website and the club app at raidernationgolfclub.com. This policy covers both.

What we collect

  • Your member account: name, email, phone, chapter, membership status, and the GHIN number and birth date the club uses to confirm it's you when you set up your sign-in. Your password is stored scrambled, never as plain text.
  • Your golf: rounds, scores, handicap, event scorecards, side games you set up and Raider Bucks activity.
  • The member map: the city you choose to show. Never your street address. You can hide yourself in Settings.
  • Club store orders: name, email, optional phone, shipping address, what you bought and the payment result.
  • Ticket exchange and messages: what you post and send inside the app.
  • Basic server records: like most websites, our servers note your device's internet address and the time of each request, to keep the service running and stop abuse.

Your location

The scorecard uses your phone's GPS to show yardages to the green and measure drives. That happens on your phone. Your live position isn't sent to the club. Your browser asks your permission first, and you can say no.

Card payments

Cards are entered on our payment company's secure page, not ours. We never see or store your full card number. We get back the result: paid or not, the amount and a transaction number.

How we use it

  • Run your membership, sign-in and chapter.
  • Keep scores, leaderboards, handicaps and Raider Bucks.
  • Fill and ship store orders and send receipts.
  • Send club news and event updates. You can ask us to stop non-essential emails at any time.
  • Keep the app safe and fix problems.

Who sees it

  • Other members see your name, chapter, the city you show on the map, event scores and leaderboard spots, and anything you post.
  • Club and chapter leaders see member details they need to run the club, like contact info and dues status.
  • Companies that help us run the club handle data only to do that job: website hosting (GitHub), our servers (DigitalOcean), maps (Google Maps), email (Google and Resend), card payments (Elavon), and the Raiders schedule (ESPN). Pages also load fonts from Google Fonts, which sees your internet address like any website does.
  • The law: if a court order or the law requires it.

We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't share it for anyone's advertising.

On your phone

The app saves some things on your phone so it works on the course with weak signal: your sign-in, rounds in progress, the courses you've opened and your store details for next time. Clearing your browser's site data removes them.

Do Not Track

We don't track you across other websites, so there's nothing for a browser's "Do Not Track" setting to turn off. We treat every visitor the same whether it's on or not.

How long we keep it

We keep your account while you're a member. Golf history and Raider Bucks stay with your account so your stats carry over year to year. Store orders are kept as business and tax records. Ask us and we'll delete your account, except what we have to keep by law.

Your choices

  • See, fix or download what we hold about you.
  • Delete your account.
  • Hide yourself on the member map in Settings.
  • Stop non-essential club emails.

Email ross@raidernationgolfclub.com and we'll answer within 30 days. We won't treat you any differently for asking.

Age

The club is for adults 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you think we have, email us and we'll delete it.

Keeping it safe

Every page and server uses an encrypted connection, sign-ins expire, and only club leaders can see member contact details. No system is perfect. If we ever find a breach that affects you, we'll tell you.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For big changes, we'll tell members in the app or by email first.

Questions about this page? Email ross@raidernationgolfclub.com.
